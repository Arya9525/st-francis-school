Curriculum Overview

A CBSE Curriculum, Reimagined

Our curriculum follows the National Education Framework under the CBSE board, enriched with international best practices. From play-based learning in the nursery to career-oriented streams in senior secondary, every stage is thoughtfully designed for the developing mind.

Learning is organised around real-world contexts, with formative assessments, project work and experiential activities woven into every term.

Academic Wings

Every Stage, Perfectly Crafted

Play, Discover & Grow (Age 3-5)

Our nursery wing is a world of colour and wonder. The play-way method, Montessori activities and sensory learning help little learners explore the world joyfully.

  • Storytelling, rhymes and phonemic awareness
  • Motor-skill development and art exploration
  • Music, movement and nature walks
  • Safe, carpeted, age-appropriate classrooms

Building Strong Foundations (Classes I-V)

Strong literacy and numeracy are built through engaging, activity-based lessons. Concepts come alive with experiments, projects and digital tools.

  • English, Hindi and Computer Science
  • Environmental Science through discovery
  • Art, music, dance and physical education
  • Value education woven into daily life

Curiosity Into Concepts (Classes VI-VIII)

Subjects deepen with critical thinking and inquiry. Students gain 1:1 devices, learn coding and robotics, and develop strong study habits.

  • Science, Maths & Social Science labs
  • Third language: French or Hindi
  • Robotics, AI & coding curriculum
  • Mentor groups for personal growth

Board-Level Rigour, Student-First (Classes IX-X)

Structured preparation for the CBSE board examinations, with personalised mentoring, mock exams and stress-management support.

  • Doubt-clearing clinics after school
  • Sample-paper and board-exam coaching
  • Career guidance & psychometric mapping
  • Leadership through student council

Pathways to the Future (Classes XI-XII)

Choice-based subject streams prepare students for competitive examinations and global universities with expert faculty.

  • Science: PCM / PCB with optional CS & IP
  • Commerce: Accounts, Business Studies, Economics
  • Humanities: Psychology, Pol. Sci., Sociology
  • JEE / NEET / CUET foundation support
