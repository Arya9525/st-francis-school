Mission and Vision

We consider school as a powerful agent in forming fully alive human beings to bring about a just society. Our school’s aim is to create individuals who are intellectually competent, morally sound, psychologically whole, imbued with the sense of divinity, committed to the cause of justice, love and peace and ever open to further growth.

The school aspires towards creating a humane society, free from prejudice, superstitions and discrimination based on sex, religion, caste and economic status, and characterized by respect for the dignity of the human, leading to concern for each other especially the underprivileged.

For the attainment of this goal, we expect parent and teacher to share this vision and co-operation in making it a reality.

Our vision of education is based on the conviction and has a unique role to play in one’s integral growth as well as in the society’s development and reconstruction.
