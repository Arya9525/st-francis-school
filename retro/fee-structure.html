Fee Structure

  • Every pupil shall pay fees for 12 months.
  • The school fees are to be paid in advance before the 20th of every month in the bank prescribed in the Fee Book.
  • Fees deposited will not be refunded.
  • A fine (late fee) of Rs. 100 will be charged after every month.
  • The payment of fees through cheques is, in no circumstances, allowed/accepted.
  • The parent shall kindly keep the receipts of fees issued to them and produce them as proof of payment, as and when needed.
  • The fees must be paid for months during which the pupil's name has been carried on in the register, even if the pupil has been absent during these months.
  • Fees will be paid in four instalments (April to June, July to September, October to December & January to March) in 'Canara Bank, Bisana, Sadabad, Hathras' (S.B. A/c No. 120001036820). The fees for the term must be paid in full, even if the pupil leaves before the term is over.
  • The fees may be paid in advance for the whole year or term.
  • Tuition may be refused to students who fail to pay the fees on the last working day of the month for which they are due.
  • No reduction of fee is made on account of mid-summer and winter holidays or other vacations or temporary absence.
  • Children whose school fees fall into arrears are liable to be debarred from writing the terminal tests and attending school until the fees are paid in full. Neither reports nor transfer certificates will be issued till all accounts are fully settled.
