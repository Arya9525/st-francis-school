St. Francis Secondary School, Sadabad

St. Francis Secondary School is a minority Christian institution, was established in 29 June, 2018 by Most Rev. Dr. Albert D’Souza, the Arch-bishop of Agra. It had humble beginning with 65 students and Rev. Fr. Joseph Rodrigues was the founder Principal.

The primary aim of St. Francis Secondary School is to impart sound religious, moral and social formation to the members of the Christian community. It also imparts education to all other students in tune with the national aspirations and make them morally, socially, physically and mentally fit to become active, useful and worthy citizens of India. We believe each student is unique. Each one has a different pace of learning, which needs to be discovered by the school. Therefore St. Thomas’ School strives to understand and fulfil the individual need of the learners. We have an education system that builds the learners' character and empower them in all aspects- intellectual, physical, emotional, spiritual and social

We further aim at forming the youth of tomorrow in the value of hard work, honesty and universal brotherhood. To come out of this portal should be vibrant young men and women holding promise to the society, not given to snobbishness and bloated pride but with an indelible mark of noble character and amiable personality. They should be deeply rooted in native culture with the Global Outlook and capable of facing the challenges of modern life.
