REV.FR.STANY RODRIGUES

“An investment in knowledge pays the best interest.” - Benjamin Franklin

My heart fills with immense pleasure as I perceive the progress being made by the St.Francis school, Sadabad. I feel much contended after bearing in mind the progress the School has made by imbibing in its students value based education synergized with modern teaching-learning technology to produce a generation of well informed and emotionally sound generation during these years.

Education and literacy are the two fac es of a coin. We have to be more educated along with being literate. A literate person needs to be educated and an educated person needs to be literate. As human beings, we are people of hope, faith and conviction. We are of a strong opinion that everything is going to be alright one day and as with every problem and tribulation this too shall pass.The cooperation and association of parents, as well as all the stakeholders towards this institution, are commendable.

Your involvement enhances the functioning of the school intact and consistent. We are created in the image and likeness of God, which demands mutual respect, love and understanding of every human being.

We need to shun the walls of hateful and divisive ways. Moreover, we need to garner respect, love and brotherhood in our pursuit of life.

In this temple of learning we stress on inculcating values and virtues to make our children competent, confident and ready to face the world with courage.

Let us love one another and win the hearts of all!

With warm regards,
REV.FR.STANY RODRIGUES
Manager, St. Francis School
