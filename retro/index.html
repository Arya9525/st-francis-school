Message from the Principal

Dear Students, Parents and Well-wishers,

It is with great pride that I welcome you to St. Francis Secondary School. For nearly a decade, our institution has stood as a beacon of academic excellence, rooted in values that build character and confidence.

Our philosophy is simple yet profound every child is unique, and education must celebrate that uniqueness. We blend a rigorous CBSE curriculum with experiential learning, technology and the arts, so that our students don't just pass examinations — they discover their passions and purpose.

As we step into 2026, I invite you to be part of a community where teachers mentor, parents partner and students thrive. Together, we will nurture minds that think critically, hearts that care deeply, and leaders who serve selflessly.

Warm regards,
Rev. Fr. Suresh D'Souza
Principal, St. Francis Secondary School

About Our School

Where Excellence Meets Compassion

St. Francis Secondary School is a minority Christian institution, was established in 29 June, 2018 by Most Rev. Dr. Albert D’Souza, the Arch-bishop of Agra. It had humble beginning with 65 students and Rev. Fr. Joseph Rodrigues was the founder Principal.

Our Vision

To empower every learner to excel intellectually, lead ethically and serve society with compassion.

Our Mission

Deliver holistic, future-ready education through innovation, values and a safe nurturing environment.

  • Experiential, activity-driven curriculum for all ages
  • Dedicated 1:1 mentoring and career counselling
  • Global partnerships and international exchange programs
  • Safe, inclusive and technology-enabled campus
